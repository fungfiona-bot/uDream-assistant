=== Manual PDF page 16 ===

In the event of power failure
 • Support two arms on the side frames,  lift and move your butt to the edge of  the seat.
 • Lift your knees upwards and ease them  out.
 • Turn off the main switch and disconnect  the power cord to prevent any sudden  operation of the massage chair if the  power is restored unexpectedly.
 • When adjusting the backrest and  footrest, always ensure there is no  object under/near the footrest/seat and  keep children or pets away to prevent  injury.
 • Do not sit on the footrest when it is  raised. Any sudden heavy weight on  the footrest could cause damage to  the mechanism, accident or injury may  occur if the product tips over.
 • Ensure that the backrest returns to its  most upright position before sitting on  the product, and recline it according to  your preference.
 • Do not place fingers or objects in  the gaps between the seat and the  backrest/footrest.
 Caution
Stop operation/Upright position
 • Press the Power/Stop button [ ] to  stop the chair operation and press and  hold Reset button [ ] to return the  backrest and footrest to the default  upright position.
