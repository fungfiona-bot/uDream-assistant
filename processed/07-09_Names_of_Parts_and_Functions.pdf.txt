=== Manual PDF page 8 ===

Names of parts and functions
Control Panel - Left
Bluetooth button 
• When Bluetooth is  off, press to turn on 
Bluetooth.
• Press and hold 3 seconds  to reset Bluetooth.
Note
 • When the product is turned on or exits Standby mode, Bluetooth will be automatically  turned on.
 • If there is no Bluetooth connectivity within 4 minutes, Bluetooth will be automatically  turned off. 
 • When the product is paired with OSIM Well-Being App, all functions (excluding Power/Stop, 
Reset, and Bluetooth buttons) on the control panel will be disabled.
Warm air button 
• Press to turn on warm air.
• Press again to turn off  warm air.
Ambient light button 
• Press to toggle ambient  light programs.
• Press and hold 3 seconds  to turn off/on ambient  light.
Scent button 
• Press to turn on both  scents.
• Press again to turn off  both scents.
Shoulder position adjustment
Press [+] button to increase shoulder position by 1.
Press [-] button to decrease shoulder position by 1.
Massage intensity adjustment
Press [+] button to increase massage intensity by 1.
Press [-] button to decrease massage intensity by 1.
SkyCanopy position adjustment
Press [▲] button to move
SkyCanopy to its fully extended position.
Press [▼] button to move
SkyCanopy to its fully retracted position.

Control Panel - Right
Standby LED 
Reset button 
• Press and hold to stop  the program and reset  the chair to the Upright  position.
Power/Stop button 
• Press to E-stop massage.
• When chair is in standby  mode, press to exit  standby mode .
Backrest adjustment 
• Press and hold :  backrest raises.
• Press and hold :  backrest lowers.
Footrest adjustment 
• Press and hold :  footrest extends.
• Press and hold :  footrest retracts.
Auto massage program  buttons 
• Press and hold the  program buttons 
(I to V). Chair will move  to designated position to  start the program.
*More auto massage programs  are available in OSIM Well-Being 
App.
Lock button 
• Press and hold 3 seconds  to lock the control panels.
• Press and hold 3 seconds  again to unlock control  panels.
• When lock function is  activated, all functions 
(excluding Power/Stop  and Lock button) will be  disabled.
 • Do not operate the control panels with wet hands.
 • Do not operate the control panels using sharp-edged or hard object. It may result in  damage.
