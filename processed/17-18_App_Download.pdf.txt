Functionality of OSIM Well-Being App
• You are able to control the product through the OSIM Well-Being App.
• New features will be highlighted and made available in the App, so that the product can stay up-to-date with the latest features.
Note
• Please keep OSIM Well-Being App running at the foreground at all times when massaging.
Compatibility of OSIM Well-Being App
• Due to variations in operating systems, non-compatibility could occur. We recommend updating your mobile operating system whenever a new version is available to enjoy the new App features. The minimum requirements of mobile operating system are subjected to change without notice.
• All smart devices require a compatible wireless connectivity (Wi-Fi or cellular data networks).
• For updates on OSIM Well-Being App, please visit the respective App stores.
• Notifications of App updates are encouraged to be enabled, and it is recommended that you install all updates to ensure that the App will function correctly and is kept up to date.
